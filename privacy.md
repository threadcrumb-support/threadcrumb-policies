# Threadcrumb Privacy Policy

**Effective Date: April 25, 2026**

Threadcrumb is a Chrome browser extension that helps you save and 
organize content from AI chat conversations. This policy explains 
what data Threadcrumb handles and how.

## What Threadcrumb stores

Threadcrumb saves the following information **locally on your 
device only**, using Chrome's built-in storage API:

- The text, code, and content you choose to save from AI chat 
  conversations
- Titles and metadata about your saved items (type, tags, notes)
- The URL of the chat each save came from, so you can return to it
- Optional comments you write on chat messages
- Your settings and preferences (theme, sort order, etc.)

## What Threadcrumb does NOT do

- Threadcrumb does not collect, transmit, or store any of your 
  data on any server
- Threadcrumb does not use analytics, telemetry, or tracking
- Threadcrumb does not share your data with any third party
- Threadcrumb does not require an account or login
- Threadcrumb does not access your AI conversations except when 
  you explicitly click to save content

## Permissions Threadcrumb requests, and why

- **storage**: To save your items locally on your device
- **tabs**: To open the source chat when you click "Jump to"
- **scripting**: To inject the save button into supported AI 
  chat sites
- **activeTab**: To act only on the tab you're using when you 
  click the extension
- **downloads**: To let you export your saves as files
- **clipboardWrite**: To copy continuation prompts to clipboard
- **sidePanel**: To display the Threadcrumb interface in Chrome's 
  side panel
- **host_permissions: claude.ai**: To detect saveable content 
  on Claude conversations

## Pro features (when applicable)

Threadcrumb is currently free during early access. If a paid 
Pro tier launches in the future, additional features may include 
cross-device sync via cloud storage. If you opt into sync, your 
saves would be transmitted encrypted to our sync provider. Sync 
is opt-in and never on by default. The current free version 
transmits no data anywhere.

## Data deletion

You can delete individual items from the side panel, or clear 
all your saves at once via Settings → Clear all saves. 
Uninstalling the extension also removes all locally stored data.
